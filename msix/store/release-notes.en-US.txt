1.4.9

New
- Store installations check for official updates silently on the first launch each day, with Home-only reminders, manual checks and a Later today option.
- Downloads and installation require separate confirmation. Settings and battery histories are saved before installation, retaining startup preferences.
- About includes an official Microsoft Store review link. Reviews are optional and never affect features.
- Submit in-app device feedback and screenshots without a GitHub account, read maintainer replies and send follow-ups. Submitted content is public.
- Add generic Bluetooth battery-cache readings and support for selected VGN, DELUX, EWEADN, AJAZZ, Veekos and Logitech devices.

Improvements and fixes
- Rename the app and executable to X-Battery while retaining the Store identity, settings, battery histories and startup preferences.
- Fix taskbar battery strip flicker when opening quick view, switching windows and entering or leaving full screen.
- Improve compact device cards, DPI controls, settings groups, paths and drawing-cache memory use; fix stale drag-ordering indicators.
- Fix duplicate Bluetooth samples and devices, and feedback relay submission failures.

Support depends on model, firmware and connection. Bluetooth battery readings require data already provided by Windows; unknown charging states remain unknown. Update availability is determined by Microsoft Store.
