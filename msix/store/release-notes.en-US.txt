1.5.0

- Show discovered devices and battery readings first, then load DPI and polling-rate settings in the background.
- Improve mouse DPI presets and quick switching, and fix Rapoo configuration writes, recovery from temporary failures and state updates.
- Double-click the taskbar battery strip to open the main window; keep an entry available when battery readings are unavailable.
- Show system devices, support status and VID/PID in device feedback.
- Right-click feedback history to export, delete or resend. Deleting a record also cleans up local exports and captures with a verified association.
- Improve offline feedback, diagnostic snapshots and communication capture, add network error details and bound local storage use.
- Use the custom feedback domain and improve recovery after connection failures and rate limits.
