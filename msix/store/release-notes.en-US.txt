New
- Added battery, actual DPI stages and polling-rate capabilities for selected VGN, VXE and ATK mice.
- Added Razer battery and settings support, with DPI stage editing, switching and rate controls on reviewed connections.
- Added read-only battery, stage and rate queries for selected Lamzu and WLMouse devices.
- Added ATK N9 V2 Ultimate and MCHOSE V9 Turbo+ headset battery queries.
- Added extended Logitech DPI and polling-rate controls.

Improvements
- X/Y stage editing and rate options follow device capabilities; changes are verified by readback.
- Improved battery caching, expiry and low-battery notification handling.
- Backends poll independently and existing ROG controls are retained.

Fixes
- Fixed intermittently unknown Rapoo battery readings during continuous reports.
- Fixed temporary query failures causing disconnected status or lost readings.
- Fixed duplicate cards on devices with multiple interfaces and stale identity after replacement.

New adapters require per-model hardware validation. Support depends on model, firmware and connection. Rapoo current DPI remains read-only; stage switching is not available yet.
