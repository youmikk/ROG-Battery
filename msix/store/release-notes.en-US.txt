New
- Switch DPI stages from the tray mouse panel.
- Adjust volume, mute the microphone, and open Windows sound settings from the tray or headset details.
- Drag devices to reorder the list; the order is saved.

Improvements
- Tray window height follows expanding and collapsing device panels.
- Settings are grouped into General, Battery & tray, Data & storage, and About.
- Improved alignment, text clarity, and synchronization of audio controls.

Fixes
- Fixed a possible crash after reordering devices.
- Fixed occasional unintended main-window activation on a single tray click.
- Fixed pauses, trails, and leftover controls during panel animation.
- Fixed clipped headset selection text.
