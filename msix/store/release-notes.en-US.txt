New
- The taskbar battery strip now shows multiple devices at once. A single device is number-only; multiple devices include a type glyph and percentage.
- Added battery support for five VGN / VXE 2.4 GHz keyboard receivers.
- Expanded support for selected Rapoo, VGN / VXE / ATK, MCHOSE, AULA, AJAZZ, Dareu, Edifier, 8BitDo, HECATE, Logitech, Razer, Sony, HyperX and CHERRY devices.
- Added Chinese and English interfaces with a language selector. Follow system is the default.
- Added low-battery Windows notifications, audio controls, device ordering and basic DPI stage switching.

Improvements
- The taskbar battery strip now uses system-tray-sized content and keeps every visible device entry whole within the available taskbar space.
- Improved recovery after sleep, cache expiry, wake-up and temporary query failures.
- Brand backends read independently while existing ROG controls are retained.

Fixes
- Fixed intermittent unknown battery readings on selected receivers, stale identities after device replacement and duplicate cards.
- Fixed crowded device glyphs and percentages in the taskbar strip.

Support covers selected devices with implemented protocols and depends on model, firmware and connection type.
