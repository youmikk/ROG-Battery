1.4.8

New
- Separate 48-hour battery charts and runtime estimates for supported mice, keyboards, headsets and controllers from all brands. Confirmed charging reports also support time-to-full estimates.
- Coarse levels retain the ≈ indicator and step charts; rounded estimates appear after enough changes are observed.
- A multi-device taskbar battery strip with quick controls, device feedback with optional diagnostics, and 1–4 sorted DPI presets on supported mice.
- Expanded support for selected Rapoo, VGN / VXE / ATK, MCHOSE, AULA, AJAZZ, Dareu, EWEADN, Edifier, MOONDROP, XIBERIA, FIFINE, Lofree and CHERRY models.

Improvements and fixes
- Histories and forecasts are isolated by device. Sleep, disconnection, charging changes and long gaps start separate intervals; cached readings are not counted twice.
- Improved history migration, device identity continuity, storage changes, clearing history and saving on exit.
- Improved taskbar icons, charging indicators, clicks, ordering and text layout.
- Retained ROG settings, Windows audio controls, Chinese and English, and Store low-battery notifications.

Support depends on model, firmware and connection. Estimates need actual samples and vary with use. Feedback opens GitHub in the browser when no feedback service is configured.
