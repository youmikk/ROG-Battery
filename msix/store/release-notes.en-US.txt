1.4.8

New
- Supported mice, keyboards, headsets and controllers from all brands now have separate 48-hour battery charts and runtime estimates. Confirmed charging readings also support time-to-full estimates.
- Coarse battery levels retain the ≈ indicator and step charts, with rounded runtime estimates after enough changes have been observed.
- The taskbar battery strip shows device types and percentages for multiple devices; click to open quick controls.
- Added device feedback and optional diagnostic previews. Builds without a configured feedback service open GitHub in the browser.
- Expanded battery support for selected Rapoo, VGN / VXE / ATK, MCHOSE, AULA, AJAZZ, Dareu, EWEADN, Edifier, MOONDROP, XIBERIA, FIFINE, Lofree and CHERRY models.
- Mice supporting numerical DPI writes can save 1–4 software presets, sorted by value.

Improvements and fixes
- Samples, storage and forecasts are isolated by device. Sleep, disconnection, charging changes and long gaps start separate intervals; cached readings do not create duplicate samples.
- Improved legacy history migration, device identity continuity, storage-location changes, clearing history and saving on exit.
- Improved taskbar icons, charging indicators, clicks and drag ordering; fixed alignment, corners and clipped text in About and feedback pages.
- Retained detailed ROG settings, Windows audio controls, Chinese and English interfaces, and low-battery notifications in the Store edition.

Support covers selected models with implemented protocols. Runtime estimates require actual samples and depend on device reports and usage.
