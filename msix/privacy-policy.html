中文

概述

R Battery（以下简称“本应用”）是一个在 Windows 任务栏显示无线鼠标电量的本地工具。本应用不收集、不上传、不共享任何个人信息。

我们不收集什么

  • 不收集姓名、邮箱、账号等任何身份信息
  • 不收集设备标识符、硬件序列号
  • 不收集使用行为、点击日志、崩溃报告
  • 不收集位置信息

网络访问

本应用不进行任何网络通信。它没有联网能力，也不会尝试连接任何服务器。

本机存储的数据

本应用会在您电脑上写入以下文件，全部仅存于本机：

  1. %LOCALAPPDATA%\rog-battery\history.tsv
    鼠标电量与充电状态的历史采样，用于估算剩余可用时间和充满时间。保留最近 48 小时，超出自动清理。
  2. %LOCALAPPDATA%\rog-battery\rog-battery.log
    仅在您手动开启调试模式（设置环境变量 ROG_BATTERY_DEBUG=1）时生成，用于排查问题。默认不创建。

以上文件您可以随时直接删除，删除后本应用会重新开始累积数据，不影响正常使用。

注册表

若您在右键菜单中选择“开机自动启动”，本应用会写入当前用户的启动项：

  • 绿色版：HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\Microsoft\Windows\CurrentVersion\Run
  • 商店版：通过 Windows 的 StartupTask 机制注册

该启动项可在应用内随时取消勾选移除，不需要管理员权限。

USB 设备访问

本应用通过 Windows 提供的 HID 接口与您的鼠标通信，用于读取电量和 DPI。它只读取，不修改设备配置，也不会访问其它 USB 设备的数据。

儿童隐私

本应用不面向儿童，也不收集任何年龄相关信息。

政策变更

若本政策发生变更，我们会更新本页面顶部的日期。由于本应用不收集任何数据，变更的可能性很低。

联系方式

如有疑问，请通过 Microsoft Store 产品页面的“支持”链接联系我们。

English

Overview

R Battery (“the app”) is a local utility that displays your wireless mouse's battery level on the Windows taskbar. The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

What we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, or account information
  • No device identifiers or hardware serial numbers
  • No usage behavior, click logs, or crash reports
  • No location information

Network access

The app makes no network connections of any kind. It has no networking capability and does not attempt to contact any server.

Data stored on your device

The app writes the following files, all stored locally on your machine:

  1. %LOCALAPPDATA%\rog-battery\history.tsv
    Historical samples of mouse battery level and charging state, used to estimate remaining time and time to full. Keeps the most recent 48 hours; older entries are pruned automatically.
  2. %LOCALAPPDATA%\rog-battery\rog-battery.log
    Created only when you manually enable debug mode (environment variable ROG_BATTERY_DEBUG=1), for troubleshooting. Not created by default.

You may delete these files at any time. The app will simply start collecting fresh data; nothing breaks.

Registry

If you enable “Start with Windows” from the right-click menu, the app writes a per-user startup entry:

  • Portable build: HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\Microsoft\Windows\CurrentVersion\Run
  • Store build: registered through the Windows StartupTask mechanism

You can remove it at any time by unchecking the same menu item. No administrator rights are required.

USB device access

The app communicates with your mouse through the HID interface provided by Windows in order to read battery level and DPI. It only reads; it does not modify device configuration and does not access data from other USB devices.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and collects no age-related information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Because the app collects no data, changes are unlikely.

Contact

For questions, please reach out through the “Support” link on the Microsoft Store product page.
